Frequently Asked Questions

Home / FAQ

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us on Telegram and we'll help you out.

How do I place an order?

Browse our catalog, click "Add to Cart" on any product you like, then go to your cart and click "Proceed to Checkout." Fill in your shipping details, upload your payment screenshot, and confirm your order. You'll receive an order confirmation immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept payment via KHQR. During checkout, you'll see our bank account details and a QR code. Simply make the transfer, take a screenshot of your payment confirmation, and upload it on the checkout page to complete your order.

How do I upload my payment screenshot?

During checkout, after reviewing your order summary, look for the "Payment Screenshot" upload field. Click to select your screenshot image (JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP format), then submit your order. Our team will verify the payment and process your order.

How long does order processing take?

Orders are typically processed within 24 hours after payment confirmation. You'll receive a status update, and you can check your order status anytime in your account under "My Orders."

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel your order as long as it hasn't been processed yet. Contact our support team via Telegram with your order ID to request a cancellation. Once an order has been approved and processed, cancellations may not be possible.

How do I track my order?

Log in to your account and visit the "My Orders" page from the user menu. You'll see all your orders with their current status: Processing, Completed, or Shipped. Click on any order to see more details including items purchased and payment status.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for defective or incorrect items. The item must be in its original packaging with all accessories. Please contact our support team via Telegram to initiate a return. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive the returned item.

How do I create an account?

Click the "Sign Up" or "Get Started" link in the header navigation. Fill in your first name, last name, email address, and a password. After submitting, you'll be logged in automatically and can start shopping immediately. You can also edit your profile later from your account settings.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Currently, please contact our support team via Telegram to reset your password. We're working on adding a self-service password reset feature in a future update.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we primarily ship within Cambodia. For international orders or special requests, please contact us directly via Telegram to discuss arrangements and shipping costs.

Are the products genuine?

Yes! We source our products directly from authorized distributors and trusted suppliers. Every item in our catalog is 100% genuine and comes with the appropriate warranty. We never sell counterfeit or replica products.

How does the wishlist and price-drop notification work?

Click the heart icon on any product card or detail page to add it to your wishlist. When a product on your wishlist has a price drop (current price is lower than the original price), you'll see a notification badge on the bell icon in the header. Click the bell to view all price drops and hot deals.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach us via Telegram at @Domzin168, or visit our Contact Us page for more details. We typically respond within a few hours during business days.
