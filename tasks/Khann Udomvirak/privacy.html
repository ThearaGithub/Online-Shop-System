Information We Collect

When you use ShopFlow, we collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you register
  • Profile Information: Avatar image, phone number, and delivery address you add to your profile
  • Order Information: Products purchased, payment screenshots, shipping details, and order history
  • Communication: Messages you send us via Telegram or contact forms

Payment Information

We do not store credit card numbers or banking credentials. All payment transactions are handled through your own banking app. We only store the payment confirmation screenshots you upload to verify your order. These screenshots are stored securely on Cloudinary.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To communicate with you about your orders (status updates, confirmations)
  • To provide customer support via Telegram
  • To improve our website and product offerings
  • To notify you about price drops on your wishlist items

Data Security

We take data security seriously. Your personal information is stored securely in our PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon cloud, which uses encryption at rest and in transit. Payment screenshots are stored on Cloudinary with secure access controls. Passwords are stored in the database.

However, as this is an academic project, we recommend using a unique password that you don't use for other services.

Cookies

We use localStorage (similar to cookies) to store your shopping cart items and login session in your browser. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers except when you place an order or log in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to run the website:

  • Neon — Cloud PostgreSQL database hosting
  • Cloudinary — Image storage for product images, payment screenshots, and avatars
  • Render — Web application hosting
  • GitHub — Source code repository

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and data handling practices. We only share the minimum data necessary for the website to function.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please contact us on Telegram.
