Frequently Asked Questions

Home / FAQ
How do I place an order?

Browse our catalog, click "Add to Cart" on any product, then go to your cart and click "Proceed to Checkout." Fill in your shipping details, upload your payment screenshot, and confirm your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept payment via KHQR. During checkout, you'll see bank account details and a QR code. Make the transfer and upload your payment screenshot.

How do I upload my payment screenshot?

During checkout, after reviewing your order summary, look for the "Payment Screenshot" upload field. Click to select your screenshot image (JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP), then submit your order.

How long does order processing take?

Orders are typically processed within 24 hours after payment confirmation. Check your order status anytime in "My Orders."

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel as long as it hasn't been processed yet. Contact our support via Telegram with your order ID.

How do I track my order?

Log in and visit "My Orders" from the user menu. You'll see all your orders with status: Processing, Completed, or Shipped.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days for defective items. Items must be in original packaging. Contact us via Telegram to start a return.

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign Up" in the header, fill in your name, email, and password. You'll be logged in automatically and can start shopping.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Contact our support via Telegram to reset your password. Self-service reset is coming soon.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently we ship within Cambodia. Contact us for international arrangements.

Are the products genuine?

Yes! We source from authorized distributors. Every item is 100% genuine with warranty.

How does the wishlist and price-drop work?

Click the heart icon on any product to add to wishlist. When prices drop, you'll see a notification on the bell icon in the header.

How do I contact support?

Reach us on Telegram at @Domzin168 or visit our Contact page. We respond within a few hours.
