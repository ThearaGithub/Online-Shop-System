Information We Collect

When you use ShopFlow, we collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Name, email, password when you register
  • Profile Information: Avatar, phone, delivery address
  • Order Information: Products, payment screenshots, shipping details
  • Communication: Messages sent via Telegram or contact forms

Payment Information

We do NOT store credit card numbers or banking credentials. We only store payment confirmation screenshots you upload, securely on Cloudinary.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To communicate order updates and confirmations
  • To provide customer support via Telegram
  • To improve our website and product offerings
  • To notify you about price drops on wishlist items
