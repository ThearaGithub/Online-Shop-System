How to Pay

We use KHQR (Cambodia QR) payment system. It's simple, secure, and fast. Follow the steps below to complete your purchase.

  1. Add items to your cart — Browse and click "Add to Cart".
  2. Go to checkout — Review your order summary.
  3. Make the payment — Use the bank details shown on checkout.
  4. Upload screenshot — Take a photo of your payment confirmation and upload it.
  5. Submit your order — Click "Place Order" and wait for confirmation.

QR Code Payment

You can also scan the KHQR code shown during checkout using your banking app (ABA Mobile, Wing, ACLEDA Internet Banking, etc.)

  • Open your banking app and select "Scan QR" or "Transfer"
  • Scan the QR code displayed on the checkout page
  • Verify the amount matches your order total
  • Confirm the transfer
  • Take a screenshot of the success screen
  • Upload the screenshot back on the checkout page

Screenshot Requirements

  • Format: JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP
  • The screenshot must clearly show: transaction amount, date/time, and "Success" or "Completed" status
  • File should be under 5MB
⚠️ Important: Orders without a valid payment screenshot will not be processed. If your screenshot is unclear, we'll contact you via Telegram for clarification.

What Happens Next?

  1. Order Submitted — You'll see an order confirmation with your order ID.
  2. Payment Verification — Our team verifies your payment screenshot (within 24 hours).
  3. Order Processing — Once payment is confirmed, your order moves to "Processing".
  4. Completed — Your order is prepared and marked as completed.

You can track your order status anytime from your My Orders page.
