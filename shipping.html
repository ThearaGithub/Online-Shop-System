Shipping Information

At ShopFlow, we strive to deliver your orders as quickly and reliably as possible. Here's everything you need to know about our shipping process.

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 24 hours after payment confirmation. During peak periods or promotional events, processing may take up to 48 hours.

Delivery Timeline

  • Phnom Penh: 1-3 business days
  • Other Provinces: 3-7 business days

Shipping Fees

Shipping fees are calculated based on your location and the size/weight of your order. The exact shipping cost will be displayed at checkout before you confirm your order. We offer free shipping on orders over a certain amount during promotional periods.

Note: Currently, we only ship within Cambodia. We hope to expand internationally in the future.

Return & Exchange Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority. If something isn't right with your order, we're here to help.

Return Window

You may return items within 7 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. Items must be in their original condition and packaging with all accessories included.

Eligible Reasons for Return

  • Defective or malfunctioning product
  • Item different from what was ordered (wrong model, color, or specifications)
  • Damaged item received during delivery

How to Initiate a Return

Contact our support team via Telegram with your order ID and a description of the issue. We may request photos of the item to assess the situation. Our team will guide you through the return process.

Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days. The refund will be issued via your original payment method. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Important Notes

  • Please inspect your package immediately upon delivery and report any damage within 24 hours.
  • We reserve the right to refuse returns for items that show signs of misuse or excessive wear.
  • For hygiene reasons, certain items (such as earphones or smart bands) may have specific return conditions.
  • Custom or personalized items are non-returnable unless defective.
